MITM-TimeEvolver: Smart Automated Timetable Generator

An algorithmic academic scheduling platform powered by Genetic Algorithms that models complex constraints (faculty workload caps, lab block allocations, and room availability) to generate clash-free schedules in under 2 minutes.

Engineering Architecture & Solution

01. Problem: Manual Scheduling Bottlenecks

Manual academic timetable scheduling requires weeks of coordinator effort. Overlapping instructor slots, uncoordinated department laboratories, and classroom capacity limits result in frequent human errors and room collisions.

02. Genetic Algorithm Optimization Engine

Models each timetable as a chromosome with fitness scoring based on hard and soft constraints (hard: zero faculty double-booking, room capacity check; soft: even workload distribution). Employs tournament selection, uniform crossover, and adaptive mutation.

03. Performance & Real Campus Impact

Generates conflict-free timetables for entire academic departments in under 2 minutes (down from 3 weeks of manual iterations). Won 2nd Prize at the Mini Project Expo 2025 for practical campus utility.

04. Interactive Admin Dashboard

Built with Flask, responsive web tables, and interactive configuration panels for adding faculty members, semester batches, subject allocations, and room matrices with CSV export capabilities.
