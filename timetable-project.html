Project Architecture & Briefing

01. Problem & Context

Manual timetable creation at colleges consumes weeks of intense administrative effort. Overlapping room slots, uncoordinated faculty availability, and sub-optimal lab allocations cause frequent conflicts and wasted space.

02. Genetic Algorithm Solution

Utilizes biological evolution principles (population initialization, fitness scoring, crossover, and mutation) to iteratively generate optimal schedules while strictly enforcing room capacities and workload limits.

03. Results & Impact

Reduces scheduling time from 3 weeks down to under 2 minutes. Guarantees 100% clash-free schedules across departments and earned 2nd Prize at the Mini Project Expo for real campus value.
